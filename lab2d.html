What to look for. Below a Reynolds number of about 47 the wake behind a cylinder is steady. Above it the wake starts to wobble and sheds swirls from alternate sides. The Strouhal number is the shedding frequency made independent of size and speed: frequency × width ÷ speed. For a cylinder it stays close to 0.2 over a huge range of conditions, which is why wires hum and chimneys sway at predictable rates.

The tunnel walls here are close to the object, which nudges the measured values a little above the textbook ones.
